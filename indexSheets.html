Allergen Awareness Notice

Our products are prepared in a kitchen that handles wheat, eggs, dairy, nuts (including walnuts, pecans, and cashews), soy, fish, and gluten-containing ingredients. Cross-contamination is possible.

If you or anyone in your party has a known food allergy, intolerance, or dietary restriction, please inform us before placing your order so we can advise you appropriately.

While we offer vegan and diet-friendly options, our kitchen is not allergen-free. We cannot guarantee that any item is completely free from traces of allergens.

Whisk 'N Flames Cakes & Catering accepts no liability for allergic reactions resulting from failure to disclose dietary requirements at the time of ordering.

For allergen queries, contact us directly via WhatsApp at (758) 712-8537 before ordering. Your safety matters to us. When in doubt, always ask.
